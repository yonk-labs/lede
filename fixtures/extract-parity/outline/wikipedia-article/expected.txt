1 | History | The curse of dimensionality was first described rigorously in the context of numerical analysis by Richard Bellman in the 1950s.
1 | Modern methods | HNSW is implemented in the FAISS library, the pgvector Postgres extension, and nearly every production vector database.
1 | Performance | Recent work has shown that carefully tuned HNSW and IVF-PQ indexes can achieve 95 percent recall at over fifty thousand queries per second on commodity hardware.
1 | Applications | In retrieval-augmented generation systems, large language models use ANN lookups over document embedding indexes to ground their outputs in source material.
