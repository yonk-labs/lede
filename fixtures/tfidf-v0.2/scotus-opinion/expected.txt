In 2019, petitioner's affiliate, Cascade Processing LLC, received a National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permit authorizing the discharge of up to eighteen tons per year of dissolved metals into the Columbia River basin. Our task is to read the statute Congress wrote. Held: Section 412(b) does not authorize civil penalties for permitted discharges that comply with a facility's unmodified NPDES permit, even if those discharges exceed effluent limits adopted by subsequent rulemaking.
