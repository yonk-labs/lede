Design proposal for an Async Event Pipeline v2 to replace a three-year-old pipeline that has grown from 50,000 to nearly 800,000 events per minute, now consuming 40 percent of infrastructure spend and generating the most on-call pages. Four goals: decouple ingest from processing, shard writers for horizontal scale, add explicit backpressure semantics, and consolidate three queuing systems into one. Proposed architecture routes events through an HTTPS gateway into a Kafka topic partitioned by customer ID, with three consumer groups (warehouse, WebSocket fan-out, alerting). Capacity target is 3x current peak (~75,000 events/second), requiring six Kafka brokers, nine partitions per topic, twelve consumers per group, and 2TB/day storage at 90-day retention. Migration happens in four phases — shadow traffic, 10 percent real traffic, 50 percent, then full cutover — over three to four months. Schema changes and the analytics read path are out of scope. Primary risks: data loss during cutover, unexpected consumer behavior, and cost overrun.
