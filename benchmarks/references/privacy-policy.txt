Acme Cloud Platform's privacy policy (effective 2026-01-01) describes collection of account data (name, email, billing, uploaded content) and automatic usage logs (IP, browser, pages, timestamps), used to provide the service, process transactions, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. The company does not sell personal information; it shares only with service providers, under legal process, to protect users, or in a merger or acquisition. Retention: billing and account records for seven years post-closure, usage logs for thirteen months, bucket contents deleted within thirty days of account closure. Users have GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA rights (access, correction, deletion, portability), exercisable by contacting privacy@acmecloud.example. Data centers operate in the US and EU; EU data stays on EU infrastructure by default, with international transfers governed by Standard Contractual Clauses. Material policy changes are announced to account owners at least thirty days in advance.
