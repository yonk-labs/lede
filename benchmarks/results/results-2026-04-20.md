## Notes

- Cross-machine numbers are invalid (DC-004). Re-run on the target machine.
- `rust-lede/tfidf` is measured via `rust/target/release/bench_tfidf` (inner loop runs in-process; no per-iteration spawn tax).
- `rust-lede/textrank` stays a placeholder — TextRank is out of scope for Plan 2.
- Output length differences are expected — lede/tfidf uses a char budget; others use a sentence count.
- P95 over 100 iterations is coarse; bump `--iterations` for tighter tails.
